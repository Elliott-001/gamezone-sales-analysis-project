Tableau Dashboard Documentation
1) Objective

The dashboard was designed to monitor sales performance, trends, and segmented contribution to revenue (including region, product, marketing channel) to track performance.

2) Data Source

Source: Transformed dataset from Power Query

Update Frequency: Static (for project)

3) Dashboard Structure

Top section: KPI summary card (total revenue), monthly revenue trendline and top location performance

Bottom section: Product performance breakdown

Right panel: Filter (Year)

4) Interactivity

The dashboard includes a dynamic filter by Year allowing for performance analysis comparisons across various segments.

5) Key Insights Derived

-Revenue is currently down from the december 2020 peak, with a MoM% change of -14% in february 2021.

-On a location basis, the US drives the bulk of the revenue generated accounting for just over 71% of total accumulated revenue through     the entire duration.

-The nintendo switch accounts for 47.6% of product sales, making it the product with the most quantity of units sold. However it lags       behind the 24inch 4k gaming monitor(product with the most revenue at $1.9M) in terms of revenue generated.
